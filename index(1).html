About

I'm a GIS specialist in Lansing, Michigan, and I believe a good map makes a complicated system feel legible. Most days that means stormwater and infrastructure; on others it means rivers drawn without land, saffron glowing over a desert, or a city turned into stained glass.

I'm especially interested in helping Michigan communities tell the stories hidden in their own data: the drains under their streets, the water that shapes their towns, the places people love.
